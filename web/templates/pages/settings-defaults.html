Expiry thresholds, days or share of the lifetime left

A threshold fires once strictly fewer whole days remain and a smaller share of the leaf's lifetime, whichever comes later: warning 30 days and 33 % alerts a 1-year certificate at 29 days, a 47-day certificate at 15 days and a 6-day certificate at 47 hours. An empty percentage means days only. ACME clients renew at about two-thirds of the lifetime, so renewal-managed targets warn later, and report renewal overdue once 75 % of the lifetime has passed.
